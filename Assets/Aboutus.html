Nairobi's Barbershop Since 2015

Since 2015, FadeCraft Barbershop has been a fixture on Tom Mboya Street — the go-to spot for clean fades, sharp lines, and proper old-school grooming in the heart of Nairobi CBD. Our barbers combine traditional technique with the styles you see on the streets of Nairobi today.

We're not just a barbershop — we're part of the neighbourhood. Regulars know us by name, and we know theirs.
